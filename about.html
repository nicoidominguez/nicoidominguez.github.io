About

I am a PhD student in Economics at the University of Maryland, where I work as a Graduate Research Assistant at the Center for Educational Data Science and Innovation (EDSI) with David Blazar and Jing Liu. My research focuses on applied labor and education economics. I combine structural econometric modeling with causal identification strategies to study how families, schools and labor markets shape inequality, with a focus on Peru and the US.

Before UMD, I earned an M.A. in Econometrics and Quantitative Economics from New York University and a B.S. in Economics from Universidad de Piura. I was a Graduate Summer Intern and Short-Term Consultant at the Inter-American Development Bank, a research assistant at Universidad de Piura and GRADE, and an economics consultant at Peru's Ministries of Labor and Production.

Fields: Labor Economics, Economics of Education. Secondary: Development, Public Economics, Industrial Organization.
